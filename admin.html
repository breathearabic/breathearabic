Publish deck

Reads the "Arabic Vocab" tab of your Google Sheet and finds words that aren't in the app yet. New words need an English meaning, a level and a type before they can be published. Words already in the app are never overwritten from the sheet.

Lessons

Short lessons for the Learn tab: a few sentences of story (each with its English), the key words, and comprehension questions. Learners see a lesson once it's published. A1 and A2 lessons are free; B1 and up are for members unless you tick Free.

Reading library

Texts for the Read page. Tick "Free" for texts free accounts can read; the rest are for members. Learners tap any word for its meaning and can save it to their reviews.

To make a book, give each chapter the same book title and a chapter number. Learners see the book as one item and move through its chapters in order. A free first chapter makes a good taster. Leave the book title empty for a single text.

Card audio

Makes the Listen audio for every card ahead of time, so it plays instantly for everyone. Words that already have audio are skipped, so run it again after publishing new words. If the daily audio cap is reached, it stops; run it again tomorrow to finish.

Backup

Supabase's free plan keeps no backups, so download one every week. It's a single file with every account, membership, card and answer. Keep it somewhere private, such as a folder in your Google Drive; it contains members' email addresses.
