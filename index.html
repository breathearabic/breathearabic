Questions

Do I need to read Arabic script?

Yes: Breathe Arabic teaches vocabulary, so you'll want to know the alphabet first. Most words show their vowel marks, and every word has audio.

What do A1 to C2 mean?

They're the CEFR levels used for languages worldwide, from beginner (A1) to mastery (C2). Arabic has no official CEFR word list, so the levels here are careful estimates based on how common and how abstract each word is.

Is this Fusha or a dialect?

Modern Standard Arabic (Fusha): the formal Arabic of books, news and khutbahs, closely related to the Arabic of the Quran, and understood across the Arab world.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes, any time, from your account. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and your progress stays saved.

NewLearningYoungKnown (memory lasts 3+ weeks)Tap a word to see its example and memory details.

Words come up in order of when they're due, oldest first, whatever their level, and the same word never comes back until a few others have been shown. New words are added in deck order and come back after 10 minutes and then an hour while you're learning them. Reviews are scheduled with FSRS, the memory model Anki uses, with two buttons like a pass/fail setup. Each time you remember a word, its memory gets stronger and the next review moves further out; each time you forget it, it goes back to learning. A word counts as known once its memory lasts three weeks, which usually takes three or four correct answers spread over a couple of weeks. A word forgotten six times becomes a hard word, which you can practise on its own. A new study day starts at 4am.

CEFR has no official word list for Arabic, so the levels here are estimates for learners of Modern Standard Arabic, based on how common and how abstract each word is.
